The task

Faster than the reference, correct on 14 shapes

Summary

What we ruled out, and what's next

Ruled out, mechanism understood
Pretransposed Linear weights
Won standalone (+80%). Regressed 10–44% stacked on CUDA-graph routes — an extra kernel launch per call outweighed the layout win.
Native fp16 weights
Failed correctness outright. Autocast keeps the residual stream in fp32 the whole time; casting it directly doesn't.
What's next
Apply pretransposition selectively
It never got a fair test on the non-graphed routes, where the launch-count penalty doesn't apply.
Cross-check on our original cluster
Now optional for our own confidence, not a submission blocker — RunPod is our canonical device.
